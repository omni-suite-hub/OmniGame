# OmniGame Privacy Policy (隐私政策)

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

OmniGame ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a lightweight offline mini-game collection box for Google Chrome. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Usage (数据收集与使用)
- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: OmniGame does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, browsing history, keystrokes, cookies, or personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Offline First**: All 100 mini-games run 100% locally on your computer. No external server requests or analytics trackers are used.

### 2. Permissions Explained (权限使用说明)
- **`storage`**: Used solely to store your game scores, preferences, and favorites locally on your device via `chrome.storage.local`.
- **`sidePanel`**: Used solely to display the game catalog and gameplay interface inside Chrome's built-in side panel.
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used to summon or re-center the floating game ball on the current tab when triggered from the right-click context menu.
- **`contextMenus`**: Used to provide convenient right-click shortcuts to open the side panel or summon the floating window.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing (第三方共享)
OmniGame does not share any data with third parties or external services.

### 4. Contact Us (联系方式)
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
